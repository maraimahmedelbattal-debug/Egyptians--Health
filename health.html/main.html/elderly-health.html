Egyptians' Health - Elderly

With aging, the elderly require special healthcare to maintain quality of life and prevent chronic diseases and health complications.

Most Common Health Problems

  • High blood pressure.
  • Diabetes.
  • Heart diseases.
  • Osteoporosis.
  • Impaired vision and hearing.

Tips for Maintaining Elderly Health

  • Regular medical follow-up.
  • Eating a balanced diet rich in calcium and protein.
  • Practicing light exercise such as walking.
  • Focusing on mental health and social support.

Importance of Family Support

Psychological and social support from the family significantly contributes to improving the health and psychological status of the elderly and reduces feelings of isolation.
