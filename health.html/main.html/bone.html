Health of Egypt's People - Skeletal System Diseases

The skeletal system is the framework that supports the body, protects internal organs, and helps with movement. Any defect in it may affect mobility and quality of life.

Most Common Skeletal System Diseases

  • Osteoporosis: A weakness in bone density that makes them more prone to fractures.
  • Arthritis: Causes pain and stiffness in the joints.
  • Bone Fractures: Occur as a result of falls or accidents.
  • Scoliosis: Curvature of the spine.

Symptoms of Bone Problems

  • Persistent pain in joints or bones.
  • Difficulty in movement.
  • Swelling or redness around the joint.
  • Increased susceptibility to fractures.

Prevention Methods

  • Eat foods rich in calcium and vitamin D.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Moderate exposure to sunlight.
  • Avoid smoking.
