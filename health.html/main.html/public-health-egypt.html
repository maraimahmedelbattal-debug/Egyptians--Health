Public Health in Egypt: Aspirations and Challenges towards a "Decent Life"

The health sector in Egypt has witnessed a qualitative leap within "Vision 2030", as financial allocations rose to 469 billion EGP in the 2024–2025 budget, alongside the expansion of infrastructure to include 2,700 hospitals. Presidential initiatives have also contributed to providing comprehensive medical services and reducing rates of unintended births.

Key Achievements

  • Expansion of health services to cover various governorates of the Republic.
  • Success of various health initiatives in providing free or low-cost services.
  • Reduction in the rates of certain chronic diseases and epidemic strains.
  • Widespread and modern development of hospitals and health centers.

Current Challenges

  • Population growth and its impact on the pressure over health services.
  • Shortage of specialized medical cadres in some areas.
  • Disparity in the quality of services between urban and rural areas.
  • The necessity of developing digital services and health data.

Future Plans

  • Universal rollout of comprehensive health insurance to all citizens.
  • Integration of the digital health system and linking electronic records.
  • Training medical cadres on the latest therapeutic methods.
  • Raising health awareness among citizens for disease prevention.
