Health of Egypt's People - Diabetes

Diabetes is a chronic condition that affects how the body uses blood sugar. Managing blood sugar levels is crucial to avoid health complications.

Types of Diabetes

  • Type 1 Diabetes: Characterized by a lack of insulin production.
  • Type 2 Diabetes: Associated with the body's resistance to insulin.
  • Gestational Diabetes: Occurs during pregnancy and may resolve after childbirth.

Symptoms of Diabetes

  • Increased thirst and frequent urination.
  • Persistent tiredness and fatigue.
  • Blurred vision and unexplained weight loss.

Prevention and Management

  • Follow a healthy and balanced diet.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Monitor blood sugar levels periodically.
  • Adhere to medical treatment if recommended by a doctor.
