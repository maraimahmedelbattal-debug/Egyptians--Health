Egyptians' Health - 100 Million Healthy Lives Initiative

The initiative aims to improve the public health of the Egyptian population by providing comprehensive and accessible health services, promoting health awareness, reducing chronic disease rates, and achieving physical, mental, and social integration for all citizens.

Key Achievements of the "100 Million Healthy Lives" Initiatives

During 6 years of work, the initiative achieved unprecedented numbers in serving Egyptian society:

  • Elimination of Hepatitis C: Screening 50 million citizens and treating 4 million infected patients.
  • Women's Health Support: Screening more than 46 million women for early detection of breast tumors.
  • Total Services: Providing more than 212 million diverse medical services benefiting about 94 million citizens.

Most Important Sub-Initiatives under the "100 Million Healthy Lives" Umbrella

  • Early Detection of Cancerous Tumors: Includes lung, colon, prostate, and cervical tumors.
  • Renal Impairment and Chronic Diseases: Providing periodic screening for diabetes, blood pressure, and kidney functions.
  • Maternal and Fetal Health: Protecting pregnant women from diseases and monitoring newborn health for 42 days.
  • Genetic Diseases for Newborns: Screening children for early detection of 19 rare genetic diseases.
  • Lung Health Initiative: Focusing on respiratory diseases and supporting smoking cessation.
