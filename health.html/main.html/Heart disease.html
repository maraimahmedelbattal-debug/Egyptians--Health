Cardiovascular Diseases

About the Diseases

Cardiovascular diseases include a group of conditions that affect the heart and blood vessels, such as high blood pressure, atherosclerosis, heart failure, and heart attacks. These diseases are considered among the leading causes of death worldwide, which makes prevention and early detection essential.

Common Symptoms

  • Chest pain or pressure.
  • Shortness of breath during activity or rest.
  • Palpitations or rapid heartbeat.
  • Swelling in the feet and ankles.

Prevention Methods

  • Following a healthy diet low in fat and salt.
  • Exercising regularly for at least 30 minutes daily.
  • Controlling weight, cholesterol, and sugar levels.
  • Avoiding smoking and alcohol.

Importance of Periodic Check-ups

Periodic check-ups contribute to the early detection of any problems in the heart or blood vessels, helping to reduce the risks of serious complications such as heart attacks and strokes.
