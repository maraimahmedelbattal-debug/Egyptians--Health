Egyptians' Health - Universal Health Insurance

Universal Health Insurance aims to provide integrated healthcare to all citizens, ensuring their access to essential and advanced medical services regardless of their social or economic status.

Objectives of Universal Health Insurance

  • Providing comprehensive medical services to all citizens.
  • Ensuring coverage for chronic and incurable diseases.
  • Improving the quality of healthcare and reducing disparities between regions.
  • Reducing treatment costs for individuals through government support.

Provided Services

  • Routine medical check-ups and early detection of diseases.
  • Medical consultations and treatment at contracted public and private hospitals.
  • Providing essential medications and medical supplies.
  • Monitoring pregnancy, childbirth, and children's health.

How to Benefit from the Initiative

All citizens can register for Universal Health Insurance through health units and hospitals affiliated with the program, presenting the insurance card to access all services.
