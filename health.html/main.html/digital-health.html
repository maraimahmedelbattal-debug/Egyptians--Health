Digital Health Initiatives in Egypt

The state seeks to develop the healthcare system through digital transformation by linking hospitals electronically and activating the unified medical record, which contributes to facilitating services and improving the quality of healthcare.

Key Pillars of Digital Health Transformation

  • Launching the electronic Universal Health Insurance system.
  • Activating an electronic health record for each citizen.
  • Automating primary healthcare centers and units.
  • Linking medical laboratories and radiology centers electronically.

Digital Services Provided to Citizens

  • Booking medical appointments online.
  • Checking lab results via the internet.
  • Monitoring health status through mobile applications.
  • Submitting complaints and suggestions electronically.

Goals of Digital Health Initiatives

  • Reducing waiting times in hospitals.
  • Raising the efficiency of medical services.
  • Achieving equity in the distribution of healthcare services.
  • Improving data quality to make accurate health decisions.
