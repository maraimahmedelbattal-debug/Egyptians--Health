Egyptians' Health - Schistosomiasis & Hepatitis C

Schistosomiasis

Schistosomiasis is a parasitic disease transmitted through contaminated water, affecting the liver and bladder.

  • Transmitted through contact with contaminated canal and drainage water.
  • Causes abdominal pain and sometimes blood in the urine.
  • Can be treated with medications available for free at health units.
  • Prevention involves avoiding contaminated water and improving sanitation.

Hepatitis C Virus (HCV)

Hepatitis C is a viral disease that affects the liver, and it was widespread in Egypt for a long time before treatment campaigns were launched.

  • Transmitted through contaminated blood.
  • Symptoms may not appear initially.
  • Causes liver damage if left untreated.
  • Millions of Egyptians were treated under the "100 Million Healthy Lives" initiative.
