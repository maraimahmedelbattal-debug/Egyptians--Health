Egyptians' Health - Early Detection of Anemia, Obesity, and Stunting

This initiative aims to prevent common diseases among children and adolescents, providing early screening to reduce complications of anemia, obesity, and stunting, and to improve public health.

Initiative Objectives

  • Detecting and treating early anemia in children to avoid negative impacts on growth and learning.
  • Monitoring obesity cases and taking preventive and nutritional measures.
  • Tracking growth to diagnose stunting and implementing early intervention for treatment.

Key Provided Services

  • Conducting routine blood tests for anemia screening.
  • Providing nutritional counseling for children and adolescents.
  • Evaluating growth and recommending treatment when needed.
  • Raising awareness among parents regarding healthy nutrition and proper development.

Benefiting from the Initiative

The initiative is available to all schools and health units across the governorates, targeting all children aged 3 to 18 years to ensure comprehensive and continuous monitoring of their health.
