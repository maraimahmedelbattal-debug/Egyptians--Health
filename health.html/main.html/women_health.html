Egyptians' Health - Women's Health

The Women's Health initiative aims to provide comprehensive health services to Egyptian women, including early detection of chronic and cancerous diseases, and promoting health awareness and prevention.

Initiative Objectives

  • Early detection of breast and uterine tumors.
  • Pregnancy monitoring and prenatal check-ups.
  • Providing necessary vaccinations for women.
  • Promoting health education and awareness about chronic diseases.
  • Facilitating access to healthcare services for women in urban and rural areas.

Key Achievements So Far

  • Screening more than 46 million women for early detection of breast tumors.
  • Providing health counseling for pregnant women and new mothers across various governorates.
  • Organizing awareness campaigns for women regarding nutrition, blood pressure, and diabetes.

Sub-Initiatives under the Women's Health Umbrella

  • Early Detection of Breast Tumors Initiative: Comprehensive screening for women over 40 years old.
  • Maternal and Fetal Health Initiative: Monitoring pregnancy and providing necessary care for mothers and newborns.
  • Health Awareness Initiative: Educational campaigns on chronic diseases and the importance of periodic check-ups.
  • Women's Mental Health Support Initiative: Providing psychological support services and counseling.
  • Reproductive Health Promotion Initiative: Providing family planning services and raising awareness of women's health rights.
  • Lung Health Initiative: Focusing on respiratory diseases and supporting smoking cessation.
