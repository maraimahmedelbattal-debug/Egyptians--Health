Respiratory Viruses - Causes, Symptoms, and Prevention

Respiratory viruses are a group of viruses that infect the lungs and upper respiratory tract, causing symptoms such as coughing, fever, and shortness of breath.

Key Respiratory Viruses

  • Influenza virus.
  • RSV (Respiratory Syncytial Virus).
  • Coronavirus (COVID-19 and similar strains).
  • Various common cold viruses.

Symptoms of Viral Infection

  • Persistent or dry cough.
  • Fever and high temperature.
  • General fatigue and exhaustion.
  • Difficulty breathing or chest tightness.

Prevention and Control Methods

  • Washing hands frequently with water and soap.
  • Wearing masks in crowded places.
  • Staying away from infected individuals when possible.
  • Adhering to seasonal vaccines such as the influenza vaccine.
