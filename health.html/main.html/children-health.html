Health of Egypt's People - Children's Health

Children's health is one of the top healthcare priorities, as proper nutrition and regular medical follow-ups significantly affect a child's healthy physical and mental growth.

Key Aspects of Child Healthcare

  • Adherence to the basic vaccination schedule.
  • Balanced nutrition rich in vitamins and minerals.
  • Regular monitoring of height and weight.
  • Encouraging physical activity and play.

Common Childhood Diseases

  • Colds and influenza.
  • Anemia caused by iron deficiency.
  • Chest allergies/Asthma.
  • Diarrhea and gastroenteritis.

Prevention Tips

  • Wash hands regularly.
  • Provide healthy and varied meals.
  • Reduce intake of sweets and carbonated drinks.
  • Consult a doctor when any abnormal symptoms appear.
